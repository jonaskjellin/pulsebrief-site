Attackers Use Passkey Phishing to Hijack Microsoft Cloud Accounts — Over 1 Million Scam Emails Sent


Sep 13, 24:00


Microsoft has disclosed two active campaigns in which threat actors abuse third-party email infrastructure for financial fraud and use passkey-themed social engineering to breach cloud environments. The first campaign sent over one million scam emails between August 3–5, impersonating CEOs; the second uses passkey phishing to compromise Microsoft cloud accounts and exfiltrate data. Passkeys were widely marketed as phishing-resistant — this campaign demonstrates that social engineering around the passkey enrollment flow remains an effective attack vector.


Passkey phishing defeating a credential type marketed as phishing-resistant is a significant threat model update for any organization that has deployed or is evaluating passkeys as a Microsoft cloud security control.
