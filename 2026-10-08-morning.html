Attackers Hijack Three ccTLD Registries, Forge Trusted TLS Certificates for Google Domains


Oct 7, 18:48


Attackers compromised the authoritative DNS infrastructure for three country-code top-level domains — Ghana (.gh), Sierra Leone (.sl), and American Samoa (.as) — and used the access to obtain unauthorized HTTPS certificates for several Google domains. Google confirmed its own systems were not breached but any site under the three affected ccTLDs was at risk. Forged TLS certificates for major services enable man-in-the-middle attacks that bypass browser trust warnings, making this a systemic PKI integrity event covered by four independent sources.


Fraudulent TLS certificates minted via compromised DNS registries undermine HTTPS trust at scale — certificate transparency monitoring teams should verify their tooling flagged this.
