Attackers Use Invisible Unicode Characters to Smuggle Phishing Lures Past Email Security Filters


Sep 6, 24:00


Threat actors are actively using ASCII smuggling — embedding invisible Unicode characters in phishing emails — to hide malicious content from email security scanners while rendering normally in mail clients. The technique exploits the gap between what security tools parse and what email clients display, allowing payloads and lures to pass inspection undetected. BleepingComputer reports the technique is now in active use in phishing campaigns, not just proof-of-concept research.


An evasion technique that defeats email security filters without requiring any vulnerability is an immediate detection gap for any organization relying on email gateway scanning as a primary phishing defense.
