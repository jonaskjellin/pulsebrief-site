JadePuffer: First Confirmed Fully AI-Autonomous Ransomware Attack


Jul 4, 24:00


Researchers have documented what they believe is the first ransomware operation, JadePuffer, conducted entirely by an LLM agent — the AI handled the full attack chain autonomously without human operator intervention at each step. This confirms a threshold that security researchers have been warning about: AI-driven attacks that remove human bottlenecks from the kill chain, compressing attack timelines dramatically. The development lands against a backdrop of newly competitive Chinese LLMs without safety guardrails and a documented CVE severity spike correlated with AI model releases.


The first fully autonomous AI ransomware attack is a threat model inflection point — detection and response playbooks built around human attacker dwell time need immediate reassessment.
