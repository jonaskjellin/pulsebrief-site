Dropbox Breached via Lenovo ID Authentication Flaw — 5,000 Accounts Compromised


Sep 2, 24:00


Attackers exploited a weakness in Lenovo's email verification process to register fraudulent Lenovo IDs, then used those to gain unauthorized access to approximately 5,000 Dropbox accounts. The incident exposes a systemic risk in federated identity: cloud platforms that implicitly trust third-party identity providers without additional account-level verification can be compromised without ever touching Dropbox's own authentication stack. Dropbox has severed the Lenovo ID integration and is urging affected users to reset credentials.


Third-party IdP trust without secondary verification is a design flaw that affects any platform using federated sign-in — a direct supply chain identity risk pattern.
