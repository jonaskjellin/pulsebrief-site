COLDCARD Hardware Wallet RNG Flaw Now Linked to $88.6M Bitcoin Theft — Scope Larger Than Initially Reported


Aug 2, 21:14


Updated reporting from BleepingComputer raises the estimated Bitcoin stolen via the COLDCARD firmware RNG vulnerability to $88.6 million, up from the $70M figure reported in the previous brief window. The flaw routed seed generation to a flawed software PRNG instead of hardware entropy, leaving affected wallets cryptographically predictable and enabling attackers to sweep thousands of addresses in minutes. Any COLDCARD user who generated wallet seeds on affected firmware should treat those wallets as compromised.


The theft figure has grown — any organization or individual holding Bitcoin on COLDCARD hardware needs to act now on migration.
