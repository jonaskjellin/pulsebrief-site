PHP patches 18 flaws, including 7 critical RCE bugs; phpAfterShell bypasses the October PHP2Shell fix


Nov 19, 16:18


The PHP Foundation shipped security releases for all supported branches, covering 18 flaws. Seven are critical and could lead to remote code execution, including CVE-2026-115560. Separately, Depthfirst disclosed phpAfterShell (CVE-2026-126714), which abuses file-based session handling and can reach unauthenticated RCE even on servers patched for PHP2Shell. Several critical bugs need non-default settings or uncommon setups, so exposure varies.


PHP runs a huge share of the web, and the new fix chain arrives on top of last month's PHP2Shell patching, so this is a patch-now item.
