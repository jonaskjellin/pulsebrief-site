Six Pre-Auth RCE Zero-Days Found in Supermarket Refrigeration Controllers — No Patch Yet


Nov 15, 15:23

Exploitation could allow attackers to manipulate cold chain infrastructure at scale, spoiling food or disrupting pharmaceutical storage.
