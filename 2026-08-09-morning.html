X Replaces Revenue Sharing Program With 'Original Content Rewards' — New Eligibility Bars Most Creators


Aug 8, 16:34


X is shutting down its existing creator revenue-sharing program on September 8th and replacing it with 'Original Content Rewards,' which requires at least 500 verified followers and 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the prior 90 days — a threshold that excludes the majority of current earners. Payouts will be based on 'qualified impressions' from Premium subscribers, not overall reach. The shift, covered by both The Verge and TechCrunch, is the latest in a series of monetization overhauls since Musk's acquisition and further centralizes value around X Premium subscriptions.


X's monetization restructuring materially changes the economics for content creators and any brand or media organization treating X as a distribution and revenue platform.


Sources: The Verge · TechCrunch
