Compromised ConnectWise Credential Used to Breach 173 Microsoft 365 Tenants


Oct 17, 06:16


Unknown threat actors used a stolen Microsoft 365 credential belonging to ConnectWise Cloud Backup (formerly SkyKick) to sign into 173 M365 partner and customer tenants, exploiting the trusted backup application's pre-granted wide permissions across customer environments. The attack demonstrates how a single compromised MSP vendor credential can cascade into a mass breach of downstream customers without any additional exploitation. ConnectWise Cloud Backup is specifically designed for managed service providers, meaning the 173 affected tenants are likely SMB and mid-market organizations relying on MSPs for IT services.


A single vendor credential compromise unlocking 173 customer M365 environments is the supply chain attack pattern MSPs and their customers need to treat as an active threat — any organization using ConnectWise Cloud Backup should audit recent access logs immediately.
