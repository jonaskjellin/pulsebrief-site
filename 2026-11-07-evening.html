$133M French Bank Heist via Service Provider — Crédit Mutuel Arkéa Forces System Suspension

Hackers breached Crédit Mutuel Arkéa through a third-party service provider, enabling fraudulent transactions totaling up to $133 million and forcing the French cooperative bank to suspend affected systems.
