CVSS 10.0: LXD and MicroCloud Flaw Lets Attackers Impersonate Trusted Clients Without Private Key


Nov 1, 08:51


A critical (CVSS 10.0) vulnerability in Canonical's LXD and MicroCloud allows an attacker to authenticate as a trusted client using only that client's public certificate — no private key required. An attacker with network access to the HTTPS API can gain full cluster access, including control over virtual machines, by impersonating any trusted account. The flaw affects a widely-used open-source container and private cloud platform, making it a high-priority patch target for any organization running LXD or MicroCloud infrastructure.


A perfect-10 flaw enabling unauthenticated cluster takeover on Canonical's container infrastructure is an immediate patching priority for any team running LXD or MicroCloud.

UK Landlines Switch Off in 100 Days — Openreach Confirms No Extensions to January 31, 2027 Deadline


Nov 1, 09:04


Openreach has confirmed that the UK's legacy public switched telephone network (PSTN) will be permanently retired on January 31, 2027, with no further deadline extensions. Any household or business still relying on copper landline telephony, including alarm systems, payment terminals, and fax machines dependent on PSTN, must migrate to VoIP or alternative services within 100 days. The cutover affects millions of endpoints across the UK, with particular risk for elderly users and businesses with PSTN-dependent infrastructure they may not have identified.


A hard 100-day deadline with no extensions for UK PSTN shutdown is immediately actionable for any organization with UK operations — unidentified PSTN-dependent systems will go dark on February 1.
