Llama-Modchip Runs 284B DeepSeek on 64GB RAM With No GPU — 161 HN Points


Nov 8, 10:22


A new open-source project called llama-modchip enables running DeepSeek's 284 billion parameter model on commodity hardware with 64GB of RAM and no discrete GPU, drawing 161 Hacker News points and 53 comments. The approach uses aggressive quantization and CPU offloading to bring frontier-class inference within reach of developers who cannot access GPU clusters. If the technique generalizes, it significantly lowers the hardware bar for running large models locally — accelerating the shift away from hosted inference that has been a recurring HN theme.


Running a 284B model on consumer RAM without a GPU is a meaningful capability threshold — it accelerates local inference as a viable alternative to cloud AI for cost- and privacy-sensitive workloads.

World Models Explained: The Next Layer of AI Moving From Text to Physics-Aware 3D Simulation


Nov 8, 10:00


Wired provides a primer on world models — AI systems that simulate real-world physics and spatial relationships rather than processing text — and argues they are becoming the foundational layer for robotics and autonomous vehicles. Unlike language models, world models must maintain coherent representations of objects, causality, and time, making them significantly harder to build but potentially more capable for physical tasks. The piece frames world models as the missing link between current AI capabilities and embodied agents that can reliably operate in the real world.


World models are the architectural bet underlying most serious robotics and autonomous vehicle programs — understanding the concept is prerequisite to tracking where physical AI is heading.
