TrojPix: Air-Gapped Systems Can Leak Data via Video Cable Radio Emissions


Jul 6, 08:50


Researchers at Shandong University demonstrated TrojPix, a technique that exfiltrates data from air-gapped computers by subtly modulating on-screen pixels to induce decodable radio emissions from the video cable — invisible to the human eye but detectable by a nearby receiver. Air-gapped systems used in critical infrastructure, defense, and high-security environments are the primary concern.
