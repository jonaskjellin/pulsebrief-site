Cyber Security


Todoist breached: 6% of users' account records stolen, plus integration tokens


Nov 25, 15:20


Todoist says an attacker got into its production systems on November 23 and downloaded account records for about 6% of users. The data includes email addresses, account creation dates and older password hashes, not plaintext passwords. The attacker also took integration tokens and reached some data in connected Outlook mailboxes. TechCrunch and Cyber Security News both cover it.


Stolen integration tokens extend the damage into connected mail accounts, so Todoist users should revoke integrations and reset passwords.


Sources: TechCrunch · Cybersecurity News
