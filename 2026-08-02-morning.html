Coldcard Hardware Wallet Firmware Flaw Linked to $70M Bitcoin Theft — 1,082 BTC Drained in 41 Minutes


Aug 1, 24:00


A March 2021 firmware integration error in Coldcard hardware wallets routed seed generation to a deterministic software PRNG instead of a hardware entropy source, leaving affected wallets cryptographically vulnerable. On July 30, an attacker exploited this to sweep 1,196 Bitcoin addresses in 41 minutes, stealing 1,082.65 BTC worth approximately $70.2 million. Galaxy Research mapped the coordinated sweep and tied it to the firmware flaw in Coldcard devices made by Canadian firm Coinkite. Any Coldcard user who generated a seed on affected firmware may have a compromised wallet.


A hardware wallet flaw that silently undermined seed entropy for years — and just enabled a $70M theft in under an hour — is an urgent signal for any organization or individual holding Bitcoin on Coldcard hardware.
