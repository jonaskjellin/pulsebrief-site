MikroTik Routers Being Hijacked via Internet-Exposed SSH With No Authentication Required


Sep 6, 24:00


Attackers are exploiting MikroTik routers with SSH exposed to the internet to gain full administrative control without authentication, with successful attacks confirmed from at least September 2, per CERT Polska. MikroTik devices are ubiquitous in ISP, enterprise edge, and SMB environments globally, making the attack surface enormous. No victim count has been published, and no patch or workaround details were specified in the warning — making firewall-level SSH restriction the immediate mitigation.


Unauthenticated remote root on one of the world's most deployed router platforms is an active, exploitable threat requiring immediate action for any network running internet-exposed MikroTik SSH.

Leap Seconds Being Phased Out — UTC Could Drift Up to One Hour From Astronomical Time


Sep 6, 24:00


International timekeeping authorities are moving to eliminate leap seconds, with a plan to allow UTC to drift from astronomical time by as much as a full hour over the coming decades — a response to the unprecedented risk of a negative leap second that current infrastructure cannot safely handle. The decision affects every system that synchronizes to UTC, including financial markets, telecommunications, GPS, and distributed computing infrastructure. The change eliminates a known operational risk (leap second bugs) while introducing a slower-moving but larger eventual correction requirement.


Eliminating leap seconds removes a known outage risk but creates a long-term UTC drift that any system with hard dependencies on astronomical time alignment will eventually need to address.
